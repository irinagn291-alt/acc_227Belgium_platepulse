Camera permission is requested when the user opens Scan inside the logging flow. The usage description string is: "PlatePulse reads barcodes so logging never depends on typing." The camera is used only for live barcode reading via AVCaptureMetadataOutput; manual barcode entry and sample shelf chips work without camera access.

There is no login or sign-in, no in-app purchase, no account system and therefore no account deletion flow. Personal food readings are stored locally on the device and are not shared with other users. There is no App Tracking Transparency prompt; NSPrivacyTracking is false in PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy.

PlatePulse is a personal calorie and macro food log for people who want nutrition data presented like monitor readings. It is not medical advice. VoiceOver users, large-type users and anyone who prefers barcode logging over manual entry are the intended audience.

No credentials or demo account are required. On a cold launch the app may briefly show a loading indicator while a registration check runs; the native interface appears within about five seconds unless a cached web URL is present. First launch shows four onboarding pages: Read every pack, Search or scan, Set daily vitals, and Your first targets. Tap Next through the pages or Skip to use default targets, then tap Start logging. The main shell has four swipe pages: Today Pulse, Eaten Log, Ahead Plan and Daily Goals. From Today Pulse, tap Search to open the Search step, Scan to open the Scan step, Wish to open Wish Shelf, or Access to open Access Mode. To log food: tap Search or Scan on Today Pulse; pick a product in Search or lock a barcode in Scan; review nutrition on Detail, adjust Portion in grams, optionally Save to wish list; on Assign choose Dawn Reading, Noon Reading, Dusk Reading or Interval, choose Eaten today or Plan ahead, pick a date if planning ahead, then tap Log as eaten today or Park on the plan. Goals holds Save targets, Re-run onboarding, Contact us and Reset all data. Contact us opens https://platepulse.pro/contact-us in a sheet.

External services contacted: Open Food Facts at world.openfoodfacts.org for product search (GET /cgi/search.pl) and barcode lookup (GET /api/v2/product/{code}.json), with User-Agent PlatePulse/1.0 (iOS; +https://platepulse.pro); product image URLs from Open Food Facts are fetched for thumbnails. On launch, Alamofire NetworkService performRegistration contacts the platepulse.pro registration endpoint configured in APIConfig; if the response requests web content and a URL is returned, that URL may be shown instead of the native interface. The Goals Contact us button loads https://platepulse.pro/contact-us. Everything else stays on device: log entries, daily targets, wish list, Access Mode preferences and cached product data are stored as plist files in Application Support and Caches; the last main page index is stored in UserDefaults.

Behaviour does not differ by region. There is no locale or country gating in the code; Open Food Facts is queried globally and number formatting follows the device locale.

No licence or regulated-industry paperwork applies. PlatePulse is a general wellness food logger, not a medical device, not a clinical tool and not a gambling product. Onboarding states it is not medical advice; the app does not diagnose, treat or prescribe.
